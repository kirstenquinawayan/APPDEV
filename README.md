## My Favorite Movie: *Titanic*  

**Genre:** Romance / Drama  

### Plot Summary  
*Titanic* tells the story of **Jack Dawson** and **Rose DeWitt Bukater**, two passengers from different social classes who fall in love aboard the ill-fated RMS Titanic. Their love story unfolds as the ship faces its tragic sinking.  

### Main Characters  
- Jack Dawson — a kind and adventurous artist  
- Rose DeWitt Bukater — a wealthy young woman longing for freedom  
- Cal Hockley — Rose’s controlling fiancé  

### Favorite Quote  
> “I’m the king of the world!” — Jack Dawson  

### Why I Love It  
- Beautiful and emotional love story ❤️  
- Stunning visuals and music  
- Based on a real historical event
